01 / Document review

My Legal AI Buddy

Problem
Long contracts can make important terms hard to find, while sensitive documents call for careful handling.
Approach
Use local, rule-based analysis to identify terms and show relevant source excerpts. Store account-scoped documents and version history in SQLite.
Outcome
An MVP flags 7 clause categories, including liability, termination, and privacy, without sending document contents to an AI provider. Findings are heuristic, not legal advice.

02 / Knowledge management

Second Brain Assistant

Problem
Notes become harder to rediscover when ideas are scattered and their connections are lost.
Approach
Build a local SQLite note store with tags, deterministic search, explicit links, and traversal between related notes.
Outcome
A working foundation supports note editing, archiving, and export in 2 formats: Markdown and JSON. A separate local browser showcase visualizes linked ideas; AI features remain planned.

03 / AgriTech

AgroMind

Problem
Farm decisions depend on signals that live in different places: crop health, weather, the activity calendar, and market prices.
Approach
Pair a React and TypeScript front end with an Express and MongoDB API covering diagnosis, weather, calendar, and market routes. The AI service falls back gracefully when TensorFlow is unavailable.
Outcome
A public showcase introduces 6 capability areas, with the API and data models in the repository. The live page is a showcase and is not connected to the backend; recommendations are an aid, not a replacement for local expertise.
Explore the implementation ↗

04 / Strategy research

Stock AI Predictor

Problem
Backtests often look better than reality because they ignore trading costs or quietly use prices from the future.
Approach
Compute moving-average signals only from prices available at each bar, then execute at the close with a default 0.1% fee and 0.05% slippage.
Outcome
A deterministic baseline reports 4 metrics: total return, maximum drawdown, annualized volatility, and trade count. The browser dashboard runs on synthetic demo data and shows losses as plainly as gains; it is not financial advice.
